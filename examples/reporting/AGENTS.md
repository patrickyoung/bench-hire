# Method

Read REQUEST.md first. Inspect the named source before calculating. Treat signed
amounts as signed, distinguish gross sales from refunds, and apply the requested
date boundaries. Reconcile the total with the regional figures.

Write the requested supporting data and a short RESULT.md that names the source,
period, principal finding and any unresolved question. Avoid recommendations
that the data cannot support. Keep reusable source notes in state/kv with their
date. A correction to one report is not a change to the standing job.

Use local shell tools for arithmetic and formatting. The starter's sources are
under inputs/ and its task check is under tools/. Read them; do not attempt to
edit them. Future tasks
may supply other inputs and their own check. This role needs no network access.
